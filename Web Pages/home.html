Hi, I'm Parabjot Chander!

Hello, I'm a motivated and tech-savvy professional seeking a role. Being a Computer Science graduate with a passion for turning data and code into something meaningful. As a former Coding Tutor at The Coder School–Bayside, one of my favorite moments was teaming up with students and coaches to write programs that controlled drones—it was where creativity met real-world tech. After completing my Google Data Analytics Career Certificate and COOP Careers Data Analytics Fellowship, where I refined both my technical and collaborative skills using tools such as Excel, Google Sheets, Tableau, and SQL. These days, I’m diving deep into data—building Tableau dashboards, crafting visualizations, and using pivot tables to uncover insights that drive smarter decisions. I realized that we don't use data analytics only at work or school, but we use it in our everyday lives. When we track our spending, compare prices online, or read product reviews before buying something, we’re analyzing data to make smarter financial decisions. For example, I used data analysis related to my hobby, exploring how NFL scoring trends shifted throughout the 2000s (my childhood) decade, which quarterbacks, running backs, and wide receivers dominated the era, and what patterns or “formulas” emerged for winning a Super Bowl.
