Hi, I'm Parabjot Chander!

Hello everyone! I'm a motivated, technology-driven professional with a background in Computer Science and a genuine passion for using technology, data, and programming to create meaningful solutions. I'm always eager to learn, explore new ideas, and apply my skills in ways that make a positive impact.

I began my professional journey as a Coding Tutor at The Coder School – Bayside, where I discovered how rewarding it is to combine technology with creativity and collaboration. One of my favorite experiences was working alongside students and fellow Code Coaches to develop programs that controlled flying drones. It was an exciting opportunity to see programming come to life through hands-on projects, demonstrating how creativity and technology can work together to bring ideas into the real world.

Currently, I work as a Receiving Associate at Burlington Stores Inc., an off-price retailer whose core values emphasize driving results, building trust, and treating others with respect. In this role, I interact with customers, take the time to understand their needs, and provide helpful recommendations and guidance. For example, when a customer asked for advice on purchasing sportswear for basketball, I recommended Dri-FIT apparel based on its moisture-wicking properties and suitability for physical activity. In addition to customer service, I process incoming merchandise by applying price and security tags (back of house), ensuring products are accurately prepared and ready for placement throughout the store. This experience has strengthened my communication, problem-solving, attention to detail, and customer service skills.

Outside of work, I'm also a YouTube content creator behind Defending Rebounding Scoring Needed (DRSN). My channel primarily focuses on NBA history from 2000 to the present, featuring player comparisons, analytical commentary, and alternative perspectives on mainstream sports media narratives. I present much of my content in a classroom-inspired, lecture-style format using slides to break down basketball topics, encourage critical thinking, and make complex discussions more engaging. Beyond basketball, I also create educational content covering both blue-collar and white-collar career topics, as well as literary and cinematic discussions centered on movies and books.

Ultimately, I'm passionate about continuous learning, creative problem-solving, technology, and sharing knowledge with others. Whether I'm writing code, helping a customer, collaborating with students, or developing educational content, I enjoy turning ideas into something meaningful. I'm excited to continue growing professionally, expanding my technical skill set, and pursuing opportunities where I can combine my interests in technology, data, and innovation.
